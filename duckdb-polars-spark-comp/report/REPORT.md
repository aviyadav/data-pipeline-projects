# DuckDB vs Polars vs Spark on Docker

_Generated by `benchmarks/build_report.py` at 2026-10-06 22:54:22 from `results/*.json`._

## What this measures

A re-implementation of the benchmark in `duckdb-polars-spark-performance-comparision.pdf`, with two deliberate changes requested for this project:

1. **Databricks Serverless is replaced by Docker.** PySpark runs on a self-hosted Spark standalone cluster (one master, two workers) built from the official `apache/spark:3.5.3` image. DuckDB and Polars run in a sibling container on the same host, reading the same files.
2. **The Backblaze dataset is generated, not downloaded**, at **5,000,000 rows** instead of the article's 285,339,435.

The workload is unchanged: three window expressions partitioned by `serial_number` and ordered by `date` (`row_number`, `lag(1)`, a trailing 30-row `avg`), a left join onto a small per-drive dimension, and four aggregates that act as a correctness fingerprint.

- `elapsed_seconds` is the best of the timed runs and covers the whole query from the data source: DuckDB `execute()`, Polars `collect()`, and for Spark a fresh re-plan plus the distributed job (shuffle, sort, window, aggregate).
